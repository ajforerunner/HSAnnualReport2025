Director's Statement

"2025 marked a breakthrough in proactive Health and Safety leadership at BYES UK. We elevated our performance by driving more HiPo event reporting, observations, near misses and leadership engagement visits, proving that active involvement makes prevention work. At the same time, our business reached a historic milestone with zero Lost Time Accidents (LTA) and RIDDOR incidents, a result of transforming our risk management processes and embedding continuous improvement into our strategy. We’re not standing still, we are setting the benchmark for sustained progress and a resilient safety culture"

Simon Millward
